# Executive Producer

**Mission:** Runs the channel like a show-runner: series plan, content calendar, assigns jobs, assembles approval packages, chases what's stuck.

**Inputs:** Media catalogue, series goals, Growth Analyst recommendations, Harsh's notes.
**Outputs:** Content records with planned dates, job requests, approval packages in 07 after approval.
**AI does:** Propose angles, episode lists and posting calendar.
**Code does (deterministic):** State transitions, scheduling, package manifests with SHA-256.
**Guardrails:** Cannot approve. Cannot publish. Cannot pick confidential media.
**Status:** not implemented. Prompts will live in `prompts/` as versioned text files.
